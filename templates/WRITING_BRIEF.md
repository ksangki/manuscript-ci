# Writing Brief

## Reader
- Who is this book for?
- What problem are they trying to solve?

## Voice
- Preserve the author's natural rhythm and vocabulary.
- Prefer concrete practitioner language over generic consulting prose.
- Do not rewrite a good sentence just to make it smoother.

## Evidence
- Do not infer causality from correlation, distribution, or ranking alone.
- Keep claims within the scope of the cited source.
- Do not create facts, numbers, quotations, or personal experience.
- Weaken absolute language when the evidence does not support it.

## Consistency
- Keep defined terms stable across chapters.
- Keep dates, intervals, thresholds, stage names, and counts consistent.
- Respect concept ownership recorded in DEDUP_DECISIONS.md.

## Editing rule
- Prefer surgical edits.
- The original wins ties.
