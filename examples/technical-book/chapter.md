# Chapter 4. A ruler should measure what the reader can act on

A maturity model is useful only when its result can change the next decision. If the diagnosis asks only about things the team cannot influence, the score becomes a description of frustration rather than a guide to action.

This does not mean every item must be directly controlled by a team leader. Some problems can be escalated with a concrete request; others are long-term infrastructure conditions. The review should distinguish those two cases instead of pretending they are the same kind of gap.
