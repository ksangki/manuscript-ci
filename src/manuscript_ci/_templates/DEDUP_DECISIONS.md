# Dedup Decisions

Record which chapter owns each major concept. Later chapters may reference it, but should not re-introduce it as new unless the new context materially changes the argument.

Example:

- Chapter 2 owns the discovery of the model gap.
- Chapter 5 owns the design rationale.
- Chapter 7 owns self-assessment bias and safeguards.
- Chapter 10 owns handover as an operational diagnosis.
- Chapter 13 owns re-diagnosis cadence and choosing the next action.
