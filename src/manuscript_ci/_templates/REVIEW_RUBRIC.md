# Manuscript Review Rubric — 100 points

## 1. Evidence discipline — 20
- Claims stay within source scope.
- No causal inference from correlation unless supported.
- No invented facts, numbers, quotes, or author experiences.

## 2. Logical consistency — 18
- Conclusions follow from premises.
- Examples do not overstate the general rule.
- Definitions remain internally coherent.

## 3. Cross-chapter consistency — 16
- Terms, stage names, numbers, dates, cadences, and thresholds match.
- A later chapter does not contradict an earlier rule without saying why.

## 4. Author voice — 16
- Distinctive wording and lived-experience texture are preserved.
- The prose does not become generic, corporate, or AI-smoothed.

## 5. Deduplication — 12
- Major ideas have one clear home.
- Repetition is purposeful rather than accidental.

## 6. Reader usefulness — 10
- The reader can act on the argument.
- Abstract claims are supported by concrete decisions, examples, or next steps.

## 7. Clarity — 8
- Sentences are readable and precise.
- Complexity is reduced without flattening nuance.

## Hard gates
A candidate is rejected regardless of score if it:
- invents a fact, citation, number, experience, or quote;
- makes a claim stronger than the evidence;
- breaks a defined term or cross-chapter rule;
- materially weakens the author's voice;
- rewrites meaning solely for stylistic smoothness.
